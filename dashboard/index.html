Where the category multiple comes from

For each category, the median market-cap / TVL ratio of the snapshot's live-token protocols in that category, shrunk toward the global median by sample size: weight n / (n + 6), so a category with twenty live protocols mostly sets its own price and one with three mostly takes the global median; categories with fewer than three live protocols take the global median outright, and every live ratio is winsorized into the 0.08–0.60 band before the median, so one outlier never sets a price. What the board covers

Reading the board

  • Rank 1 is the highest implied value. Values fall as rank rises.
  • The WATCHLIST badge marks the snapshot's own watchlist: at least $50M of TVL and no token on record.
  • The DEPLOYMENT badge marks one versioned deployment (a V2 or V3) of a pre-token protocol, discounted to 70% — a slice of a protocol, not the whole of it.
  • Rows below the floor are the early radar — smaller pre-token protocols the watchlist leaves out.
  • Exchanges stay off the board, and so do wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade at or above the floor: ranking one of those first would be ranking Bitcoin or ETH, not a launch.
  • Relative momentum is a percentile: a protocol's week ranked against its own category, so one outlier's wild week cannot stretch its neighbours' scores.
  • Farm plans are eight concrete steps with a cost and a time, plus an effort rating and an estimated cost per row. Never pay a fee to claim anything.
